# Entrance flow

Landing OBLIVIA link → /arrival/ → /station/ (Primary Docking Bay).

`docking.mp4` is the supplied 12.07-second, 1278 × 720 clip. `arrival.json` configures the source. Original desktop file remains untouched.

Muted inline autoplay, pause/resume, skip, retry after errors, and manual play for reduced-motion visitors are supported. Missing configuration shows a still with a manual continue link. The final fade reveals the preloaded bay artwork; the destination keeps that artwork visible while its existing datapad boot runs.

Scene ambience is explicitly enabled per page, with a mute toggle and saved volume. Procedural audio supplies irregular low pulses, ventilation noise, and data chirps. Optional quiet synthetic names use browser speech synthesis; this approximates whispers and varies by installed voice. No recorded whisper asset was available. Audio stops when hidden or navigating away. Enable again in the next scene to respect browser audio permissions.

Changes are local to progression-repo. Deploy through the existing site's normal hosting process.
