Daedalus // Mission continuity

Active Directives

DIRECTIVE 01
MAINTAIN ORBITAL POSITION
Kepler-III must maintain stable orbital position above Europa. Station drift, orbital decay, or unauthorised repositioning is prohibited unless commanded through authorised command channels.
PRIORITY: STANDARD
DIRECTIVE 02
PRESERVE STATION CONTINUITY
All crew are responsible for maintaining operational continuity of Kepler-III systems, archives, and cryogenic assets. Loss of continuity is considered a mission-level failure condition.
PRIORITY: ELEVATED
DIRECTIVE 03
REPORT SYSTEM ANOMALIES
Any irregularities in station behaviour, navigation routes, time perception, memory continuity, or environmental readings must be logged and reported through the Mission Log and Diagnostics Hub immediately.
PRIORITY: ELEVATED
DIRECTIVE 04
RESTRICTED ACCESS PROTOCOL
Access to sealed sections, archival vaults, and non-listed sub-decks is restricted. Any crew member encountering an unlisted corridor, structure, or deck designation must report immediately and not proceed alone.
PRIORITY: RESTRICTED
DIRECTIVE 05
CONTINUITY OF RECORD
Mission logs must be maintained even in the event of conflicting records, missing time, or inconsistent system reports. If records do not match lived events, both must be preserved.
PRIORITY: ARCHIVAL
